The Vision

It Began with a Vision

The search was never simply for a property. It was for a building with a soul — one carrying authentic Balinese craftsmanship, human touch and a story worth preserving.

Rather than demolish what stood here, the residence was carefully transformed. Original character was retained, restored and given room to live comfortably in the present.

The Residence

A Residence Designed to Breathe

Wide openings allow the house to move naturally between inside and out. High ceilings, shaded terraces and open sightlines welcome the breeze, while carefully considered materials keep every room calm and comfortable.

Traditional Balinese carving and craftsmanship sit easily beside contemporary comforts. Nothing feels staged; each element belongs to the rhythm of the residence.

The Living Pavilion

Nothing Was Selected to Impress
Everything Was Selected to Belong

The living pavilion is the social heart of Swarna Mandapa — a place for coffee at first light, long conversations and evenings that unfold without a timetable.

At its centre, the imported Italian marble island is both beautifully crafted and entirely useful: a natural gathering point where meals are prepared, stories are shared and guests always seem to find one another.
